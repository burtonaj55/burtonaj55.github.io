Lessons of the Line - Online football coaching program

Lessons of the Line is an online coaching program. Through my website I can have direct lessons with football players who play offensive line. Players from middleschool to college are often not taught offensive line because its not widely talked about. My program will allow me to teach technique, scheeme, or even just the inside information I have for aspiring players.
